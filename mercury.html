MERCURY

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun. It has a cratered rocky surface and huge temperature changes between day and night.

About Mercury

Rocky Planet

Mercury is a terrestrial planet with a rocky, heavily cratered surface.

Closest to the Sun

Mercury travels around the Sun closer than any other planet in our Solar System.

Temperature Changes

Mercury experiences very large temperature differences between its day and night sides.
