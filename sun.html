THE CENTER OF OUR SOLAR SYSTEM

THE SUN

The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System. Its energy provides the light and heat that make life possible on Earth.

Explore the Sun

About the Sun

A Star

The Sun is a star made primarily of extremely hot plasma, mostly hydrogen and helium.

Produces Energy

Nuclear fusion in the Sun's core releases enormous amounts of energy that eventually reaches the planets as sunlight.

Essential for Earth

Solar energy drives Earth's climate, weather and many biological processes.

Sun Facts

Type Star
Star Class G-type Main-Sequence Star
Diameter About 1.39 million km
Age About 4.6 billion years
Distance from Earth About 150 million km
Main Elements Hydrogen & Helium

Solar Flares

The Sun can release powerful bursts of energy called solar flares. Solar activity can also affect the space environment around Earth.

Solar Wind

The Sun continuously releases a flow of charged particles called the solar wind, which travels throughout the Solar System.
