Shape & Structure

Earth is an oblate spheroid: slightly wider around the equator than from pole to pole. Beneath the surface are the crust, mantle, outer core and inner core.

🌊

Water World

Liquid water covers most of the surface. Water constantly moves through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff, groundwater and ocean circulation.

🌬️

Protective Atmosphere

The atmosphere provides gases needed by life, helps regulate temperature, creates weather and shields the surface from much harmful radiation.

Earth's Major Systems

Four connected parts work together like one giant system.

🪨

Geosphere

Rocks, minerals, soil, mountains and Earth's interior. Plate tectonics continuously reshape continents and ocean basins.

💧

Hydrosphere

All of Earth's water: oceans, rivers, lakes, glaciers, groundwater, soil moisture and water vapor.

☁️

Atmosphere

The envelope of gases surrounding Earth. It drives weather and climate and interacts with oceans and land.

🌿

Biosphere

All living organisms and the ecosystems where they interact with air, water, rocks and each other.

🔥

Energy Balance

Earth receives energy from the Sun and sends energy back to space. Greenhouse gases affect how efficiently heat escapes.

🔄

Connected Cycles

The carbon, water, nitrogen and rock cycles move materials around the planet and connect distant ecosystems.

Climate & Atmosphere

Climate is the long-term pattern of weather.

What is the greenhouse effect?

Sunlight warms Earth's surface. The surface releases infrared energy, and greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit some of that energy. This natural process keeps Earth much warmer than it would otherwise be.

Water vapormajor natural greenhouse gas
Carbon dioxideimportant long-lived gas

Why climate matters

Climate influences where ecosystems can thrive, water availability, agriculture, sea level, extreme heat and many other parts of human and natural systems.

Weather describes conditions over short periods. Climate describes patterns over much longer periods.

Human activities that add greenhouse gases to the atmosphere are changing Earth's energy balance and warming the planet.

Life on Earth

From microscopic organisms to enormous ecosystems.

🌳

Forests

Forests store carbon, support biodiversity, influence rainfall and provide food, materials and habitat.

🪸

Oceans

Marine ecosystems contain extraordinary biodiversity. Oceans also store large amounts of heat and carbon.

🐝

Pollinators

Bees, butterflies, birds, bats and other animals help many plants reproduce, supporting food webs and agriculture.

🦠

Microbial Life

Microorganisms drive decomposition and nutrient cycles and live in nearly every environment on Earth.

🧬

Biodiversity

Biodiversity includes genetic diversity, species diversity and the variety of ecosystems.

🌱

Photosynthesis

Plants, algae and some microorganisms use light energy to make organic matter, releasing oxygen in the process.

💧 The Water Cycle

Water continuously circulates between ocean, atmosphere, land and living things. Solar energy drives evaporation; cooling causes condensation; precipitation returns water to the surface.

🌳 The Carbon Cycle

Carbon moves among the atmosphere, oceans, rocks, soils and living organisms. Photosynthesis removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere, while respiration, decomposition, fires and volcanic activity return carbon.

🌋 Plate Tectonics

Earth's rigid outer shell is divided into moving plates. Their interactions build mountains, create ocean crust, cause earthquakes and power much volcanic activity.

🧲 Earth's Magnetic Field

Motion of electrically conducting material in the outer core generates Earth's magnetic field. It extends into space and helps deflect charged particles from the solar wind.

How Can We Protect Earth?

Small choices and large systems both matter.

🌱

Restore Nature

Protect forests, wetlands, grasslands, mangroves and other habitats. Restoration can improve biodiversity and ecosystem services.

⚡

Use Cleaner Energy

Improving energy efficiency and shifting toward low-carbon energy can reduce greenhouse-gas emissions.

♻️

Reduce Waste

Use resources efficiently, reuse products when possible and recycle materials through effective local systems.

🚲

Smarter Transport

Walking, cycling, public transportation and efficient vehicles can reduce pollution and energy use.

💧

Save Water

Fix leaks, use water efficiently and protect watersheds, rivers, groundwater and wetlands.

📚

Learn & Share

Understanding Earth systems helps people make better decisions and distinguish evidence from misinformation.
