⚠️ What does this ranking actually mean?

These figures are not simply the pollution produced inside a company's buildings. Carbon Majors attributes emissions connected with fossil-fuel and cement production, including emissions from the combustion of marketed products under its methodology. Therefore this ranking should be described as an emissions-attribution dataset, not a simple factory pollution ranking.
