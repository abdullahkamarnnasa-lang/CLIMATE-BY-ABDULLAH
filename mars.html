About Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. It is often called the Red Planet because iron minerals in its soil oxidize and create a reddish appearance.

Mars has enormous volcanoes, deep valleys, polar ice caps and evidence that liquid water existed on its surface billions of years ago.

Scientists study Mars to understand its past, present environment and whether it could have once supported microbial life.

Mars Exploration

Robotic spacecraft, orbiters, landers and rovers have explored Mars and sent valuable information back to Earth.

Rovers can investigate rocks and soil, take photographs and analyze the environment.

Future missions may help scientists understand whether humans could safely explore Mars for longer periods.
