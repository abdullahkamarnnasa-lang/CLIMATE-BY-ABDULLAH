NEPTUNE

Neptune is a distant blue ice giant known for its powerful winds, deep blue appearance, faint rings and dramatic atmosphere.

Explore Neptune

About Neptune

Ice Giant

Neptune is one of the Solar System's two ice giants. Its interior contains materials such as water, ammonia and methane under enormous pressure.

Powerful Winds

Neptune has some of the fastest winds measured on a planet in our Solar System, creating a dynamic atmosphere.

Faint Rings

Neptune has a faint ring system made from dark material. Several distinct rings are known around the planet.

Neptune Facts

Planet Type Ice Giant
Diameter 49,244 km
Day Length About 16 hours
Year Length About 165 Earth years
Atmosphere Hydrogen & Helium
Color Deep Blue
Known Moons 16

Blue Atmosphere

Methane in Neptune's atmosphere absorbs some wavelengths of red light, contributing to its rich blue appearance.

Extreme Weather

Neptune's atmosphere contains changing clouds, storms and extremely strong winds, making it one of the most active atmospheres among the outer planets.
