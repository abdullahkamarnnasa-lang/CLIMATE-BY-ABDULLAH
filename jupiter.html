About Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in the Solar System.

It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Unlike Earth, Jupiter does not have a solid surface that spacecraft could land on.

Its atmosphere contains enormous cloud bands and storms, including the famous Great Red Spot.

Jupiter Exploration

Spacecraft have visited Jupiter to study its atmosphere, magnetic field, moons and radiation.

The Juno spacecraft has provided scientists with detailed observations of Jupiter's atmosphere and interior.

Jupiter's moons are also important targets for planetary science.

The Great Red Spot

One of the most famous storms in the Solar System.

Jupiter's Giant Storm

The Great Red Spot is a huge storm in Jupiter's atmosphere. It has been observed for centuries and is much larger than Earth.

Explore Jupiter

Learn about the amazing features of the giant planet.

Cloud Bands

Jupiter's atmosphere contains colorful bands of clouds moving around the planet at extremely high speeds.

Many Moons

Jupiter has a large family of moons. Four famous large moons are Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto.

Powerful Magnetic Field

Jupiter has an enormous magnetic field that creates an intense radiation environment around the planet.

Extreme Winds

Jupiter's upper atmosphere contains powerful jet streams and rapidly moving clouds.

Faint Rings

Jupiter has a faint ring system made from tiny particles and dust.

Future Exploration

Scientists continue studying Jupiter and its moons to understand how giant planets and planetary systems form.
