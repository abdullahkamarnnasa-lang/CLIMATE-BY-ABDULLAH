Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

Understanding Carbon Dioxide and Its Role in Climate Change

What is Carbon Dioxide?

Carbon dioxide (CO₂) is a colorless gas that naturally exists in Earth's atmosphere. Plants use CO₂ during photosynthesis to produce food and oxygen.

However, human activities such as burning coal, oil and natural gas have greatly increased the amount of CO₂ in the atmosphere.

Atmospheric CO₂

~430 ppm

Atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration is now roughly around 430 parts per million.

Approximate modern value; concentration changes over time.

Main Sources of CO₂

🚗 Transport

Cars, trucks, ships and airplanes burn fossil fuels.

🏭 Industry

Factories and industrial processes release large amounts of CO₂.

⚡ Energy

Coal, oil and natural gas are major sources of carbon emissions.

🌳 Land Use

Deforestation reduces the Earth's ability to absorb CO₂.

Why Does CO₂ Matter?

Carbon dioxide is a greenhouse gas. It absorbs and re-emits infrared radiation, helping keep Earth warm.

Increasing CO₂ levels contribute to long-term global warming and climate change.

Effects of Increasing CO₂

🌡️ Global Warming

Higher greenhouse gas concentrations contribute to rising global temperatures.

🌊 Ocean Changes

Oceans absorb CO₂, which increases ocean acidity and affects marine ecosystems.

🌳 Ecosystems

Climate changes can affect forests, agriculture and wildlife.

How Can We Reduce CO₂?

  • 🌞 Use renewable energy such as solar and wind.
  • 🚲 Use public transport, cycling and walking.
  • 🌳 Protect forests and plant trees.
  • ⚡ Improve energy efficiency.
  • 🏭 Reduce emissions from industrial processes.
