What Is a Medical Robot?

A medical robot is a robotic system designed to assist with healthcare tasks. Depending on its purpose, it may use sensors, motors, cameras, computers and specialized software.

Important: many surgical robots are controlled directly by trained surgeons. A robot does not automatically replace a doctor.

Types of Medical Robots

🔬 Surgical Robots

Help trained surgeons control specialized instruments during selected procedures.

🏥 Hospital Robots

Can support delivery, transport, information and other hospital tasks.

🦾 Rehabilitation Robots

Assist supervised movement training and rehabilitation for some people recovering from illness or injury.

What Is Inside a Robot?

Common components vary depending on the robot's purpose.

01. Frame and joints

Provide structure and allow controlled movement.

02. Motors / actuators

Convert energy into movement.

03. Sensors

Measure distance, position, force or other conditions.

04. Controller

Processes inputs and runs the control program.

05. Camera

Captures visual information when needed.

06. Power system

Supplies suitable electrical power.

07. Software

Controls movement, interfaces and sensor processing.

08. Safety systems

Help detect faults and stop unsafe operation.

How Are Medical Robots Developed?

Step 1: Identify a healthcare need with medical experts.

Step 2: Design the robot using engineering and CAD tools.

Step 3: Select suitable sensors, actuators and electronics.

Step 4: Develop the control software and user interface.

Step 5: Test in simulation and controlled lab conditions.

Step 6: Conduct rigorous safety, reliability and regulatory testing.
