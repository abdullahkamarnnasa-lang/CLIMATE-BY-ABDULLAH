Methane (CH₄)

Understanding Methane and Its Role in Climate Change

What is Methane?

Methane (CH₄) is a colorless and odorless gas made up of one carbon atom and four hydrogen atoms.

Methane is a powerful greenhouse gas and plays an important role in Earth's climate system.

Atmospheric Methane

~1,900 ppb

Main Sources of Methane

🐄 Agriculture

Livestock such as cattle produce methane during digestion.

🛢️ Oil & Gas

Methane can escape during the production, processing and transport of fossil fuels.

🗑️ Landfills

Organic waste decomposing without oxygen can produce methane.

🌾 Rice Farming

Flooded rice fields can produce methane as organic material decomposes.

Why Does Methane Matter?

Methane is much more effective than carbon dioxide at trapping heat in the atmosphere over a short period.

Although methane remains in the atmosphere for less time than CO₂, reducing methane emissions can have a relatively fast climate benefit.

Effects of Methane

🌡️ Warming

Methane contributes significantly to greenhouse gas warming.

🌫️ Air Quality

Methane contributes to the formation of ground-level ozone.

🌍 Climate Change

Increasing methane emissions add to human-caused climate change.

How Can We Reduce Methane?

  • 🐄 Improve livestock management.
  • 🛢️ Detect and repair oil and gas leaks.
  • 🗑️ Capture methane from landfills.
  • 🌾 Improve rice farming techniques.
  • ♻️ Reduce organic waste.

Methane Situation

Increasing

Atmospheric methane levels have increased substantially compared with pre-industrial levels.
