URANUS

Uranus is a fascinating ice giant with a blue-green atmosphere and a unique sideways rotation. Explore this distant world and discover what makes Uranus special.

Explore Uranus

About Uranus

Ice Giant

Uranus is classified as an ice giant. Its interior contains water, methane and ammonia in extreme conditions.

Sideways Rotation

Uranus rotates with an extreme axial tilt of about 98 degrees, making it appear to spin almost sideways.

Faint Rings

Uranus has a system of faint rings. Scientists have identified many individual rings around the planet.

Uranus Facts

Planet Type Ice Giant
Diameter 50,724 km
Day Length About 17.2 hours
Year Length About 84 Earth years
Atmosphere Hydrogen & Helium
Color Blue-Green
Moons 27 known

Uranus Atmosphere

Uranus has an atmosphere dominated by hydrogen and helium, with methane giving the planet its distinctive blue-green appearance.

Extremely Cold

Uranus is one of the coldest planetary environments in the Solar System. Temperatures high in its atmosphere can become extremely low.
