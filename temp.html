Understanding Earth's Changing Temperature

What is Global Temperature?

Global temperature refers to the average temperature of Earth's surface, including both land and oceans.

Scientists track global temperature over many years to understand how Earth's climate is changing.

Current Global Temperature Change

+1.5°C

Recent global temperatures have been around 1.5°C above the pre-industrial average in some recent years.

Values vary by year and dataset.

Why Is Earth Getting Warmer?

🏭 Greenhouse Gases

CO₂, methane and other greenhouse gases trap heat in Earth's atmosphere.

🚗 Fossil Fuels

Burning coal, oil and gas releases greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

🌳 Deforestation

Removing forests reduces the amount of CO₂ that ecosystems can absorb.

Effects of Rising Temperature

  • 🌊 Rising sea levels
  • 🔥 More extreme heat
  • 🧊 Melting glaciers and ice sheets
  • 🌲 Changes in ecosystems
  • 🌾 Effects on agriculture

Global Warming Status

Rising Temperature

How Can We Help?

  • ☀️ Use renewable energy
  • 🚲 Walk, cycle or use public transport
  • 🌳 Protect and restore forests
  • ⚡ Save electricity and improve efficiency
  • ♻️ Reduce waste and recycle
